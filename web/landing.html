10 AI Intelligence Modules

Person Detection

Density Analysis
Compute people-per-area across custom zones. Classify zones as LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH density with historical trending.

Movement Analysis
Track movement intensity per zone. Detect sudden surges or stagnation that precede dangerous crowd conditions.

Speed Analysis
Measure average and peak crowd velocity in px/s. Temporal memory tracks acceleration over a rolling 5-second window.
1 credit / unit

Direction Analysis
Compute circular mean heading per zone and directional consistency (R-value). Detects converging or chaotic motion patterns.
1 credit / unit

Optical Flow Analysis
Farneback dense optical flow measures global motion magnitude and convergence zones — uncovering macro movement invisible to per-person tracking.

Compression Detection
Nearest-neighbour distance analysis identifies when people are being pushed together — a key early indicator of stampede formation.
2 credits / unit
🚪
Exit Blockage
Monitor occupancy at all exit zones. Triggers alerts when exits approach capacity, preventing bottleneck formation in emergencies.
2 credits / unit
🎭
Behaviour Analysis
LLM-powered (Groq/LLaMA 3) natural language scene analysis. Interprets crowd signals and generates human-readable cause explanations.
3 credits / unit

Risk Prediction
12-feature weighted model with EMA smoothing and linear trend regression. Outputs a 0–100 risk score with SAFE / WARNING / CRITICAL classification.
