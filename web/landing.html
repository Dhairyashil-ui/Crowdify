How it works

From camera to life-saving alert in seconds

Three simple steps connect your cameras to a real-time AI safety nerve centre.

01
📷
Connect Your Camera
Stream any IP camera or Android device to the Croudify backend. Works with existing CCTV infrastructure — no proprietary hardware needed.
02
🧠
AI Analyses Crowds
YOLOv8 + ByteTrack detects every person in real time. 10 intelligence modules compute density, compression, flow vectors, and risk scores.
03
🚨
Command and Respond
Authority dashboard shows live zone heatmaps, incident alerts with AI-recommended actions, and escalation timers. Credits are deducted per intelligence unit.
